How it works

From craving to first bite in three steps

1

Search your craving

Type the dessert by name, like tiramisu, ube cheesecake, or gulab jamun. Not just "bakery."

2

See the closest spots

Places that have it, sorted by distance from your ZIP code or where you are now, on your dessert radar.

3

Go get it, then rate it

Get directions or check DoorDash. Afterward, rate it, leave a review, and report menu changes for the next person.

Features

Every dessert, and where it is

Get the app

Put Dessert Spotter on your home screen

iPhone

  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app at the top of the page, or open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
